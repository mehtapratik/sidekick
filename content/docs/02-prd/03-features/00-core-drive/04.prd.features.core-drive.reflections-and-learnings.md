Core Drive to follow reflective writing pattern — any observed gap between core drive source and reality warrants retrospection—actual observation becomes case study to understand: what went wrong—why was there a gap in first place—what can be learned from this observation—how to update the entry in future to close the gap.

Important—Core Drive is grounded in reality—practical—takes stock of its user’s strength and weaknesses and works with it to deliver maximum impact—though a gentle, subtle nudge is towards better behavior and habit is acceptable—does not push users to some higher, ideal, moral, righteous state
