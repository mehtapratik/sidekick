The foundational layer upon which entire value proposition of Sidekick rests — holds the most private and sensitive information — user’s identity, principles, moral values, heuristics, preferences, constraints etc — defines and drives entire system’s behavior — influences how [[war-room]] plans, how [[factory]] executes, and how [[alter-ego]] reasons and synthesizes its responses.

## Nurturing growth

Iterative improvement and gradual disclosure — gets better with more usage, data and grooming

Starts with slow, negligible gains — may even demand more time than it saves during its infancy — shows exponential gains to those who trust the process, stick to it and stay consistent — the estimated timeline of visible gains around 3 to 9 months

## Your best friend in the mirror

knows you better than yourself — has access to [[information|information]] and constantly evolving [[algorithm-of-life|Algorithm of Life (AL)]] — reminds you what matters if you start wandering — helps you choose right time and place for certain behavior to improve likelihood of success — knows how to amplify your strengths while avoiding (or working around) your weaknesses
