Similarities between [[00.prd.features.core-drive.overview|Core Drive]] vs. Taxila—both store timeless information—neither stores transient and short-lived information—transient information: this years’s resolution, vacation plans, your weekly plan—timeless information: user’s preference of doing one thing at a time or their learning notes on strategic decision making and game theory.

Difference between [[00.prd.features.core-drive.overview|Core Drive]] vs. Taxila—Core Drive: selectively curated, minimal and commonplace information—Taxila: monolithic corpus of knowledge on diverse subjects consumed via top-k similarity retrieval—core drive: do one thing at a time—taxila: learning notes of strategic decision making and game theory.
