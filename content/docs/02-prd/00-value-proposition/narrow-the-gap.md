Reduce cognitive dissonance — help users align their words and actions with their beliefs, principles and dreams — a system that helps its users be successful with themselves and narrow the gap between...

1. what they have vs. what they want,
2. who they are vs. who they want to be,
3. what they say vs. what they do, and
4. their plans vs. their execution.
