A snapshot of available information at a specific moment in the past, represented as $@\{timestamp\}$. For instance, $I@20260912$ represents the state of information on September 12, 2026\. The concept of a historic state of information can also be expressed abstractly as $I@t$, which indicates that the information is historic without specifying its exact age.
