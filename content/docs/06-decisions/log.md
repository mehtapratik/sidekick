Every decision Sidekick has made, newest first. A decision is a claim about how something works, so it lives in the page about that thing; this log records when it was made and where it now lives. Dates are when the decision was first recorded. A later change to a decision adds a line to the Decision History section of its page.

The ID column links to each decision's line in its page. Rules, requirements and principles have ids too and are not logged here: the [Datum Index](/datums/) lists every datum on the site by id. A row with no ID records a page that holds no datum, such as one since retired.
