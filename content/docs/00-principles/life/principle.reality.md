Conventionally, reality refers to scientific facts and natural laws. These are the unshakable truths indifferent to people’s beliefs and ignorance. I am expanding the scope of reality to include societal beliefs, cultural normals, individual worldviews, and prevalent truths of the day and age. This expanded reality will help me come up with an operational manual to determine what is appropriate response to a situation or how to act under given circumstances.

| Types of Reality   | Natural Reality                                                                       | Societal Reality                                                                               | Transient Reality                                                                                                                        | Personal Reality                                                                                                        |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Definition**     | Scientific facts and natural laws                                                     | Societal beliefs and cultural norms                                                            | Prevalent beliefs, knowledge and sense of right and wrong by day/age                                                                     | My personal worldview, my conditions, flaws, and strengths                                                              |
| **Bias**           | Cannot be reasoned with and indifferent to everything except laws of cause and effect | Susceptible to reason and arguments. - It is possible for two conflicting reality to coexist without challenging each other:
	- Transient reality of geocentric view up to mid-16th century coexisted with Natural reality of heliocentric view.
	- Most Indians love cricket and support Indian cricket team as their patriotic duty. I don’t watch/follow cricket (or any other sport) and am completely fine being called traitor for not knowing if India won yesterday night against Pakistan or not.
- Therefore our society should learn to recognize and respect diversity of subjective reality. Learn to respect differences. Agree to disagree without challenging others’ worldview thinking that only yours is right.
- Finally, most important decision of all: when to yield to challenging reality vs. when to put down your foot and fight back to change the reality? Refer principle of [[principle.pragmatism|pragmatism]].
	- Decide: Is matter at hand worth fighting for? Is it important to me? 
		- Yes: What can I do to help others see the world I do? Am I willing to pay the price and accept the risk associated with challenging dominant views? You cannot always fight back without looking like a troublemaker and whistleblower. Therefore, choose your battles sparingly and one at a time — the one that matters to you most.
		- No: 
			- Adapt your behavior in public to match the current reality. Pushing back, denying, refusing might not work if your decisions and actions can’t influence final outcome or if you’re not in position of power. It will only hurt you when result is undesired. 
			- No one is stopping you from living your own life based on your beliefs and worldview in private. Stay private about affairs and don’t whine about opposing ideas and practices you don’t like.
