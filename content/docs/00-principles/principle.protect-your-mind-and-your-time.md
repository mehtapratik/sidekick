- Protect your mind, body and will from distractions, and non-productive and trivial tasks.
- Use [[principle.focus-funnel|focus funnel]] technique to avoid doing all things you don’t have to, leaving you with things that you either enjoy, things that matters to you, or things that has severe consequences if went sideways.
- Template to trim fat, cut noise and eliminate distractions:
	1. If things went sideways, can you afford to live with it? Can you make your peace with it? Can you tolerate the loss? 
	2. Will you have opportunity to course-correct or reverse the oucome? 
	3. Is there even a bigger fish to fry even though the matter at hand is important and you’d do yourself? 
	 If answers to any of these question is “**yes**”, then avoid doing it yourself. 
- Prioritize, plan and focus on remaining few tasks that matters a great deal to you or has severe consequences if gone sideways.
