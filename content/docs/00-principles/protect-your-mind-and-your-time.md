Offload all you can using [[principle.focus-funnel]]. Protect your mind and willpower from wearing down. Ask yourself if things went wrong and mistakes were made: 

1. Can you afford to live with it? Can you make your peace with it? Can you tolerate the loss? 
2. Will you have opportunity for course-correction or reversal (undo) of the outcome? 
3. Is there a bigger fish to fry even though the matter at hand is important and you’d do yourself? 

If answers to any of these question is “**yes**”, then you should avoid doing it yourself (unless of course, you enjoy doing it, in which case it’d be your hobby).

Prioritize, plan and focus on remaining few tasks that matters a great deal to you or has severe consequences if gone sideways.
