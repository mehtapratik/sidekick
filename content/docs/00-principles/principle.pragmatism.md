- Approach life with diplomacy and practical attitude. 
- Do not be the person who always whines and complains. Neither be someone who always see someone or something else as problem to their issues. 
- Either accept the way things are and stop complaining or challenge the present state and be a part of the solution (or movement). Those who watch from the sidelines are either hypocrites at worst or whiners at best.
- If you can manage to convince enough people (and/or people in position of power) about your version of events and story then that becomes new reality and you’ve just changed the world.
- Do not argue with fools and ignorants. Rational conversation with them is non-starter. You will either be made fun of or be ridiculed, criticized and outcast. Rational arguments and debates are only possible when opposing party has growth mindset free of ego. 
- Do not extend your ideology and morality unto others. Your “right way” of living life is just yours and extending them to others is a recipe to unhappiness and sours the relationship.
