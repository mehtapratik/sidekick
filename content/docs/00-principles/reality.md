Everyone have a concept of right and wrong, good and bad, heroes and villains.
What to do when your idea of “rightness”, “heroism”, and “goodness” does not match reality?

- Pushing back, denying, refusing might not work if your decisions and actions can’t influence final outcome or if you’re not in position of power. It will only hurt you when result is undesired.
- Decide: Is it worth fighting? Is it important to me? What can I do to change reality so it matches my vision? Am I willing to pay the price and accept the risk associated with fighting back? You cannot always fight back without looking like a troublemaker and whistleblower. Therefore, choose your battles sparingly and one at a time — the one that matters to you most.
- If answer is no, adapt your behavior to match the current reality.
- If the answer is yes, then come up with plan to change reality. It either involves problem solving (making NY city safer enough for women to walk on lonely street at 3am in the morning) or shifting the narrative by convincing enough people (right to bear arm has done more harm than good to our nation and it is in our mutual interest to surrender this right for safer schools and public spaces).
