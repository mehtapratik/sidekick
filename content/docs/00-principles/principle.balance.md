Strive to live a balanced life without overreaching on few things or people of life doing injustice to others. 

Look at everything that needs your time, attention, energy and resources. Try to find an optimal point of allocation to gain most or lose least. Start thinking of balance as optimization problem in an approximate manner (known as utility model in economics).
