# Sidekick public site

The public face of Sidekick: one site that introduces the project, publishes its writing and docs, and hands visitors off to the App. It replaces arkive.blog.

## Language

### The project

**Sidekick**:
The working name of the project and the only name the wordmark and logo carry, until a final brand name is chosen.
_Avoid_: Project Sidekick (in the wordmark), Mira, Arkive

**App**:
The signed-in Sidekick product that people use, as opposed to the public site that describes it.
_Avoid_: Dashboard, platform, product

**Public site**:
Everything a visitor sees before signing in: the home page, Writings, Docs and About.
_Avoid_: Marketing site, blog, Arkive

### Content

**Entry**:
One published page of authored Markdown, either a Writing or a Doc.
_Avoid_: Post, note, article, page

**Writing**:
An Entry written as a finished essay for readers, listed under Writings.
_Avoid_: Blog post, essay (as a section name), blog

**Doc**:
An Entry that comes from the project vault: a PRD, plan, decision, build log, glossary term or harness note.
_Avoid_: Vault note, wiki page

**About**:
The Writing "A Bird's Eye View" that introduces Sidekick; the About link opens it.
_Avoid_: Intro page, landing page
